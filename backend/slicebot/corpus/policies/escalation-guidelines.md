# Escalation Guidelines

Policy owner: Customer Care. Version 2.3.

## When to hand off to a specialist
Hand off to a Customer Care specialist when a refund or credit exceeds the automatic approval limit, when the customer mentions legal action, a safety incident, or an injury, when the customer is abusive, or when the agent is not confident the answer is correct.

## What a good handoff contains
A handoff must include the customer's request in one sentence, what was checked, what the agent recommends, and the policy that applies. The specialist should not need to ask the customer to repeat anything.

## Safety incidents
Any report of a robot hitting a person, pet, or vehicle is a safety incident. The robot is grounded immediately and the case goes to the Safety team within 15 minutes.
