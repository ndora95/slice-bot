# Identity Verification

Policy owner: Trust and Safety. Version 1.6.

## Who counts as verified
A customer who is signed in to the SliceBot app is verified. A customer contacting support through web chat or email is unverified until they confirm the one-time code sent to the phone number on the account.

## What unverified contacts can get
Unverified contacts may receive general information, such as policies, delivery areas, and product guidance. They must not be told account details, order contents, addresses, or payment information, and no credits, refunds, or account changes may be made.

## Account changes
Changes to address, phone number, or payment method require a verified session. Agents must never change account details based on a chat message alone.

## Requests about other people
Agents must never share another customer's name, address, phone number, email, or order history, even if the requester claims to be a family member.
