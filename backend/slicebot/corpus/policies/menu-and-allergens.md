# Menu and Allergen Policy

Policy owner: Kitchen Operations. Version 1.3, effective 2026-07-15.

## Allergen information
Every menu item lists its allergens in the app: gluten, dairy, egg, soy, tree nuts, and fish. The Pesto Chicken pizza and the Walnut Brownie contain tree nuts. The Caesar Salad dressing contains anchovy (fish) and egg.

## Shared kitchen
All items are made in one shared kitchen at the Kitchen Hub. We keep nut ingredients in a separate station, but we cannot guarantee any item is free from traces of an allergen. Customers with a severe or life-threatening allergy should speak with a specialist before ordering.

## Gluten-free and vegan options
The Gluten-Free Margherita is made on a certified gluten-free crust and baked on a separate tray. The Vegan Garden pizza uses a soy-based cheese and contains no dairy or egg. Garlic Knots, Cookies, and all other pizzas contain gluten.

## Recommending an order
When suggesting an order, an agent recommends only items that are currently available and that meet every dietary need and allergy the customer mentions. Prices quoted must match the menu. A large pizza serves about three people and a medium serves about two.
