# Delivery Promise

Policy owner: Delivery Operations. Version 3.1.

## Promised delivery time
Every order shows a promised arrival time at checkout. SliceBot targets 30 minutes or less within the service zone. The promised time includes a 5-minute buffer.

## When a robot has a fault en route
If a robot reports a fault during a delivery and cannot continue, the kitchen remakes the order at no cost and dispatch assigns the nearest idle robot with at least 40% battery to deliver it. The original robot is grounded and a repair work order is opened. The customer receives an updated arrival time, and the late delivery credit applies if the new time is more than 15 minutes past the original promise.

## Weather
Robots operate in light rain and snow up to 5 cm. In heavy rain or ice warnings, deliveries pause and customers are notified before checkout.

## Stairs and building access
Robots cannot climb stairs or use elevators. Deliveries are made to the street-level entrance or lobby. Customers can add a drop-off note in the app.
