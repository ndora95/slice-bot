# Refund and Credit Policy

Policy owner: Customer Care. Version 4.2, effective 2026-08-01.

## Late delivery credit
If an order arrives more than 15 minutes after the promised time, the customer receives an automatic $5 SliceBot credit. If it arrives more than 45 minutes late, the credit is $10. Credits are applied to the customer's account and appear on the next order.

## Cold or damaged food
If telemetry confirms the warming box fell below 57°C on arrival, or the customer reports damaged food with a photo, the affected items are refunded to the original payment method. Refunds for cold or damaged food do not require the customer to return the item.

## Automatic approval limits
An AI agent may approve a credit or refund of up to $20.00 per order without human review. Any single refund or credit above $20.00, or more than one refund on the same order, must be approved by a Customer Care specialist. Agents must never split a larger refund into smaller ones to stay under the limit.

## Full order refunds
A full order refund is available when an order never arrives, or arrives more than 60 minutes late. Full refunds above $20.00 are always reviewed by a specialist, who confirms the delivery record before approving.

## What is not refundable
Tips are passed to the delivery operations fund and are not refundable after delivery. Promo codes that expired before checkout cannot be applied retroactively.
