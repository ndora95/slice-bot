# Ops Bulletin OB-2026-014: Lid seal wear on batch M2-B07

Issued 2026-09-28 by Fleet Engineering. Status: open.

## Summary
Robots from manufacturing batch M2-B07 are showing early wear on the lid seal gasket. The gasket compresses at the hinge corner after roughly 1,500 lid cycles, letting heat escape during deliveries.

## How to spot it
Affected robots lose more than 8°C in the warming box during deliveries under 25 minutes. Customers report cold pizza. The heater element is working normally on affected robots.

## Action
Replace the lid seal gasket (part LS-220) on affected M2-B07 robots during off-peak hours. Do not ground robots during the dinner rush for this issue alone; it is a quality problem, not a safety problem. Refund cold-food complaints from affected robots under the standard policy.
