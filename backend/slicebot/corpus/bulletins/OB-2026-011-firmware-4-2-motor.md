# Ops Bulletin OB-2026-011: Firmware 4.2.0 motor current reporting

Issued 2026-09-15 by Fleet Engineering. Status: resolved.

## Summary
Firmware 4.2.0 reported motor current spikes that were not real. Firmware 4.2.1 fixed the reporting. All robots were updated by 2026-09-20.

## Guidance
Motor stall faults on firmware 4.2.1 or later are real and should be handled with the drive wheel motor stall procedure in the service manual.
