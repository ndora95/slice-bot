# SliceBot M2 and M3 Service Manual

Audience: repair technicians. Revision 11.

## Drive wheel motor stall
Symptom: one motor draws more than 12 A while speed is near zero, followed by fault code MTR_STALL. Cause: debris in the wheel well or a failed motor bearing. Fix: clear the wheel well; if the stall repeats, replace the drive wheel motor (part WM-110). Repair time: 45 minutes. Skill: drive.

## Battery capacity fade
Symptom: battery health below 70% or a full charge lasting fewer than 4 hours. Fix: replace the battery pack (part BP-400). Repair time: 30 minutes. Skill: battery.

## Warming box heat loss
Symptom: the warming box loses more than 8°C during a delivery under 25 minutes. Most common cause on the M2 is a worn lid seal gasket at the hinge corner. Check the heater element only after the seal is confirmed good. Fix: replace the lid seal gasket (part LS-220). Repair time: 20 minutes. Skill: seal.

## Heater element failure
Symptom: the warming box cannot reach 60°C before departure. Fix: replace the heater element (part HE-205). Repair time: 35 minutes. Skill: heater.

## Lid lock not releasing
Symptom: the app sends Unlock but the lid stays shut, fault code LID_ACT. Fix: replace the lid lock actuator (part LL-150). Repair time: 25 minutes. Skill: body.

## Camera mast damage
Symptom: obstacle camera offline, fault code CAM_LOSS. The robot must not operate without the camera. Fix: replace the camera mast module (part CM-310). Repair time: 40 minutes. Skill: sensors.

## Scheduling repairs
Repairs must not take a robot out of service during the lunch peak (11:30 to 13:30) or the dinner rush (17:00 to 21:00) unless the robot is already grounded. Parts move from the central Hub warehouse to the North and South repair depots by runner robot.
