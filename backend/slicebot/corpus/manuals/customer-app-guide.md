# SliceBot App Guide

Audience: customers. Version 2026.9.

## Unlocking the lid
When the robot arrives, open the order in the SliceBot app and tap Unlock. The lid opens for 90 seconds. If the lid does not open, wait 10 seconds and tap Unlock again. After two failed attempts, the app offers a 4-digit PIN that can be typed on the robot's keypad.

## Tracking a delivery
The order screen shows the robot's live position and the current arrival estimate. The estimate refreshes every 30 seconds. If a robot is replaced during a delivery, the app shows the new robot's number.

## Delivery zones
SliceBot delivers in four zones in Washington, DC: Capitol Hill, Eastern Market, Navy Yard, and the Southwest Waterfront. Every order is cooked at the Kitchen Hub on 3rd St SE by Canal Park. Addresses outside these zones cannot be selected at checkout.

## Changing an order
Orders can be changed or cancelled for free until the kitchen starts preparing them, usually within 2 minutes of ordering. After that, cancellation is not available.

## SlicePass membership
SlicePass costs $7.99 per month and includes free delivery on orders over $15 and priority robot assignment during the dinner rush.
